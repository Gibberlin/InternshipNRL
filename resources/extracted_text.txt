Part 1 — Industrial IT/OT Convergence & Digital Infrastructure

5G CNPN and Edge Computing
BSNL and Numaligarh Refinery signed an agreement to deploy a captive non-public 5G network (CNPN) at NRL, intended to provide ultra-reliable, low-latency connectivity for industrial use cases including real-time IoT telemetry and AR/VR-based operator training.

Private 5G CNPNs in industry enable millisecond-level latency and deterministic QoS at the site edge, which supports time-sensitive sensor telemetry, remote video for situational awareness, and immersive augmented/virtual reality applications for maintenance and training.

Deployments typically combine BSNL’s spectrum/core services with system integrators and edge compute (on-prem or MEC) to place data processing close to the plant, reducing backhaul to central clouds and improving resilience and security for critical control-plane traffic.

Hexagon 3D Digital Twin
Hexagon’s digital-twin solutions create visual, spatially accurate replicas of industrial sites that merge as-built 3D geometry with live telemetry to form a single operational picture.

Connected digital-twin workflows bind physical assets (pipes, valves, instruments) to IoT streams and enterprise systems so that condition, location, and performance metrics update in near real time; this supports predictive maintenance and operator situational awareness.

Hexagon products provide connectors and integrations for operational and cloud systems, enabling the twin to ingest sensor/IoT feeds and present a synchronized, georeferenced view of asset state and alerts.

AspenTech, SCADA/DCS and InfoPlus.21 Historian
AspenTech InfoPlus.21 (IP.21) is a high-performance process historian designed to collect millisecond-to-second time-series from DCS/SCADA/PLC systems and contextualize events for analytics, visualization, and batch/process reporting.

Typical integrations use industry-standard interfaces (OPC, REST, ODBC, COM) and are architected to stream plant automation telemetry (process variables, alarms, batch events) into IP.21 for both real-time dashboards and long-term analysis.

When paired with digital twin or analytics platforms, IP.21 supplies the live telemetry that keeps 3D models and decision-support apps in sync with the physical plant.

Cloud Migration — AWS IEDDMS for Engineering Blueprints
Enterprise engineering document and data management systems migrate to AWS to scale storage of large engineering blueprints and enable serverless or containerized processing for OCR, indexing, and metadata extraction.

On AWS the typical architecture for an IEDDMS places document storage in Amazon S3, uses Lambda/Step Functions for processing and orchestration (e.g., Textract for OCR), and persists metadata in DynamoDB or Aurora, all inside a secured VPC with IAM controls and encryption.

Hexagon and other EAM/digital-twin vendors have documented integrations and joint best-practices with AWS for securely hosting digital-twin services, enabling the twin and document systems to share artifacts, telemetry, and AI/ML services in cloud-native workflows.

Migration benefits include scalable object storage for very large drawings, automated intelligent document processing (indexing and search), disaster recovery, and simplified integration with analytics or machine-learning pipelines hosted on AWS.

How these pieces work together at NRL (concise architecture narrative)

Edge capture and transport: Field sensors, PLCs, and operator AR devices send telemetry over the BSNL private 5G CNPN to on-site edge compute or MEC nodes to guarantee low latency and site-isolated connectivity.

Local historian and control integration: Edge gateways forward millisecond-level telemetry into the plant historian (Aspen InfoPlus.21) and into SCADA/DCS supervisory layers for real-time control and alarm management.

Twin synchronization and situational awareness: Hexagon’s 3D digital twin subscribes to historian and IoT streams so the 3D site model reflects live values (flow, temperature, valve status), enabling visual alerts and predictive maintenance workflows.

Cloud-backed documentation and analytics: Engineering blueprints, vendor manuals, and large asset files are stored in AWS (S3) and processed with document-inspection and ML services; the cloud-hosted IEDDMS provides global access, versioning, and integration with asset management and twin services.

Security & resilience considerations: The CNPN isolates industrial traffic from public networks, while edge compute, historian retention policies, and cloud IAM/encryption protect data flows; system integrations should follow standards (OPC, REST) and vendor best-practices for secure connectors.

Short list of primary integration points (useful for later securing/design)

5G CNPN ↔ MEC/Edge compute (sensor aggregation, protocol translation).

Edge gateways ↔ DCS/SCADA (OPC/Modbus) ↔ InfoPlus.21 historian.

InfoPlus.21 ↔ Hexagon digital twin (real-time feeds and contextual metadata).

IEDDMS (AWS S3 + Lambda/Textract + DB) ↔ Engineering workstations and Hexagon/asset systems.

Cross-domain APIs (REST/HTTPS) used by SOC or SIEM collectors to ingest telemetry/alerts for cybersecurity monitoring.
